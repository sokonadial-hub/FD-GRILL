A family kitchen, twenty years in the making

FD Grill is a family business, rooted in our family's heritage and in the beauty of food made from whole ingredients.

For more than 20 years we've cooked the dishes we grew up with: kabobs marinated in house spices, stews that simmer for hours, pastry folded by hand. Now we're turning that experience into a place of our own. Our restaurant is being renovated for a 2027 opening, and until the doors open, we're bringing the food to you through catering.

Catering, straight off the grill

Grill favorites

  • Steak

Handhelds & street bites
  • Shawarma sandwich

    Juicy kebab wrapped in warm pita with house sauce, lettuce, tomatoes and onions.

  • Patté African beef patties

    Flaky pastry filled with spiced ground beef or chicken, boiled egg and herbs.

  • Nem West African spring rolls

    Five crispy rolls with glass noodles, mushrooms and beef or chicken. With sweet chili sauce and house salad.

Platters
  • Djouka Malian peanut couscous

    Fonio steamed with roasted ground peanuts, with a savory onion sauce and your choice of fried tilapia or a flame-grilled kabob.

  • Vermicelli slow-cooked onion pasta

    Tender pasta in a rich caramelized onion sauce, slow-simmered and served with your choice of protein.

  • Zaamè Malian jollof rice

    Short-grain rice simmered in a spiced tomato and herb sauce, served with your choice of fish or meat.

African stews

Every stew comes with white rice or fonio.

  • Peanut stew maafe

    Groundnut stew with beef, slow-cooked in peanut-tomato sauce.

  • Peanut stew with potato greens

    Groundnut and potato green stew with meat in peanut-tomato sauce.

  • Cassava leaf stew

    Ground cassava leaves cooked with smoked turkey and beef in an aromatic sauce.

  • Saka-saka potato greens

    Stewed potato greens with garlic, onions, herbs, beef and palm oil.

  • Red onion stew

    Onions and tomatoes low-cooked with vegetables, with smoked chicken.

  • Yassa

    Smoked chicken braised in onion, lemon and mustard sauce.

  • Fakoye

    Jute leaf stew with lamb or beef, seasoned with West African spices.
