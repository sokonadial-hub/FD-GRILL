20+ years at the grill. Now, a new chapter.

FD Grill is a family business, rooted in our heritage, in live-fire cooking and in the beauty of food made from whole, natural ingredients.

For more than 20 years we've cooked the dishes we grew up with: kabobs marinated in house spices and charred over the coals, stews that simmer for hours, pastry folded by hand. Now we're growing: we're building a full kitchen at our Philadelphia location and opening it for sit-down dining in 2027. Our catering carries on as always.

Off the fire

Not sure where to begin? These three are where we'd start your menu.

Kabobs

Beef, chicken or mushroom, marinated in house spices, then charred over live charcoal until the edges crisp. Built for a crowd.

Leaf stews

Cassava leaf, saka-saka or fakoye, slow-cooked with meat and served with white rice or fonio.

Nem

Crispy West African spring rolls with glass noodles, mushrooms and beef or chicken. With sweet chili sauce and house salad.

Over the coals

Marinated in house spices and cooked over live charcoal until the smoke sets in.

  • Kabob

    One charcoal-grilled skewer of beef, chicken or mushroom, marinated in house spices.

  • Kabob combo

    Charcoal-grilled skewers of beef, chicken or mushroom, with one side.

  • Grilled lamb

    Lamb marinated in house spices, charred over the coals and rested until juicy.

  • Steak

    Steak in our house seasoning, seared hard over live charcoal.

  • Lamb gigot

    Tender leg of lamb, double cooked in our signature seasoning. With one side and onion-mustard sauce.

Handhelds & street bites

Easy to pass around at parties and gatherings.

  • Shawarma sandwich

    Seasoned grilled meat wrapped in warm pita with house sauce, lettuce, tomatoes and onions.

  • Patté West African hand pies

    Flaky pastry filled with spiced ground beef or chicken, boiled egg and herbs.

  • Nem West African spring rolls

    Five crispy rolls with glass noodles, mushrooms and beef or chicken. With sweet chili sauce and house salad.

Platters for the table

Malian classics, each served with your choice of protein.

  • Djouka Malian peanut couscous

    Fonio steamed with roasted ground peanuts, with a savory onion sauce and your choice of fried tilapia or a flame-grilled kabob.

  • Vermicelli caramelized onion pasta

    Tender pasta in a rich caramelized onion sauce, slow-simmered and served with your choice of protein.

  • Zaamè Malian jollof rice

    Short-grain rice simmered in a spiced tomato and herb sauce, served with your choice of fish or meat.

West African stews

Every stew comes with white rice or fonio.

  • Maafe peanut stew

    Groundnut stew with beef, slow-cooked in peanut-tomato sauce.

  • Peanut stew with potato greens

    Groundnut and potato green stew with meat in peanut-tomato sauce.

  • Cassava leaf stew

    Ground cassava leaves cooked with smoked turkey and beef in an aromatic sauce.

  • Saka-saka potato greens

    Stewed potato greens with garlic, onions, herbs, beef and palm oil.

  • Red onion stew

    Onions and tomatoes low-cooked with vegetables, with smoked chicken.

  • Yassa

    Smoked chicken braised in onion, lemon and mustard sauce.

  • Fakoye

    Jute leaf stew with lamb or beef, seasoned with West African spices.
